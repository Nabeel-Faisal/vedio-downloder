FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is Download My Video really free?

Yes. The service is 100% free with no premium tier, no usage caps, and no signup required. We run on minimal infrastructure costs and believe video access should be open to everyone.

Do I need to install an app or browser extension?

No. Everything happens in your browser. There's nothing to install, nothing to update, and nothing to uninstall later. Works on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android.

What video formats and qualities can I download?

MP4 is the default format and works on every device. You can pick any quality the original video offers — from 144p all the way up to 4K Ultra HD. You can also extract audio as MP3 at high bitrate.

Are downloaded videos watermark-free?

Yes. Files are downloaded in their original form — no added watermarks, no service branding, no compression beyond what the source already had.

Is it legal to download videos?

You can legally download videos you own, videos with permissive licenses (Creative Commons), and videos for personal/fair use. Always respect copyright and the terms of service of the source platform. Don't redistribute downloaded content commercially without permission.

Do you store the videos I download?

No. Videos stream directly from the source site to your device — they never sit on our servers. We don't log URLs, IP addresses, or downloaded content.

Why is YouTube sometimes slower than other sites?

YouTube has the most aggressive anti-bot measures of any platform. We continuously update our system to handle their changes, but occasional slowdowns or temporary failures can happen. If you hit one, just try again in a few seconds.
