How to Download YouTube Shorts to Phone or PC (Free, 2026)

YouTube Shorts are short vertical videos — perfect for quick entertainment. But unlike TikTok, YouTube doesn't let you save Shorts from the official app. Here's the simplest free way to download YouTube Shorts as MP4 files on any device.

Updated June 19, 2026 6 min read By Download My Video

Why YouTube Shorts can't be downloaded normally

YouTube Shorts launched as YouTube's answer to TikTok. Unlike regular YouTube videos, they're vertical, capped at 60 seconds, and designed for endless scrolling. There's no built-in download button on Shorts — not even for YouTube Premium subscribers in most regions.

The good news: a Short is still a YouTube video under the hood. That means any tool that can download regular YouTube videos can also download Shorts.

How to download YouTube Shorts in 3 steps

Step 1 — Copy the Shorts URL

On phone: Open the Short in the YouTube app. Tap the Share icon (the arrow), then tap Copy link.

On desktop: Open the Short on youtube.com. Right-click the video and select Copy video URL, or copy the URL from your browser's address bar. It'll look something like youtube.com/shorts/abc123xyz.

Step 2 — Paste it into the downloader

Go to downloadmyvideo.com and paste the Shorts link into the search bar. The tool detects it's a Short, fetches the metadata, and shows you a preview with available qualities.

Step 3 — Pick MP4 quality and download

Shorts are usually available in 720p or 1080p HD. Pick your preferred quality (or just Best Quality (auto)) and click Download. The file saves directly to your device as an MP4 — no watermark, no logo, just the original vertical video.

Downloading Shorts on iPhone

The process is identical on iPhone:

  1. Open the Short in the YouTube app and tap Share → Copy link.
  2. Open Safari and go to downloadmyvideo.com.
  3. Tap and hold the input field, paste the link, choose a quality, tap Download.
  4. When the download finishes, tap the download icon in Safari's address bar, then Save to Files.
  5. To put it in your camera roll: open Files, find the MP4, tap Share → Save Video.

Downloading Shorts on Android

On Android it's even simpler:

  1. Open the Short → Share → Copy link.
  2. Open Chrome and go to downloadmyvideo.com.
  3. Paste, choose quality, tap Download.
  4. The MP4 saves to your Downloads folder. From there you can move it to Gallery or share to any app.

Why save YouTube Shorts?

People download Shorts for plenty of legitimate reasons:

  • Saving content from a creator you love — in case it gets removed
  • Reposting to other platforms (with proper credit) — Reels, TikTok, etc.
  • Editing into compilations for your own channel
  • Sending to friends who don't use YouTube
  • Watching offline without needing the YouTube app open
  • Building a personal archive of tutorials, recipes or how-to clips

Does the downloader work for old Shorts?

Yes. Any Short that's still publicly viewable on YouTube can be downloaded — even ones from years ago. Private Shorts, age-restricted content, and removed videos can't be downloaded because the source content is no longer accessible.

What quality do YouTube Shorts download in?

Most modern Shorts are uploaded in 1080p (1920×1080) or sometimes 720p, depending on the creator's phone or camera. The downloader gives you all available qualities and you pick the one you want. For Shorts specifically:

  • 1080p — recommended. Sharp on any modern phone screen.
  • 720p — fine for older devices or smaller storage.
  • MP3 — if you only want the audio (great for sound clips, memes, music snippets).

Common questions

Does the downloaded Short have a watermark?

No. YouTube doesn't add watermarks to Shorts the way TikTok does, so the file you get is the clean original — exactly as the creator uploaded it.

Will the creator know I downloaded their Short?

No. Downloads are anonymous. The creator won't see anything — no notification, no view counter change, nothing.

Can I download multiple Shorts at once?

You download them one at a time, but since the whole process takes ~10 seconds per Short, you can save a dozen in just a couple of minutes.

Is downloading Shorts legal?

For personal use (offline viewing, archiving, fair-use editing), generally yes. Don't redistribute or monetize content that isn't yours without permission from the creator. Always respect copyright.
