How to Download Instagram Reels & Videos (No Watermark) — 2026

Instagram doesn't make it easy to save videos. There's no download button on Reels, no save option for Stories from other accounts, and the only built-in option ("Save") just bookmarks the post for later — it doesn't actually give you the file. Here's the free, watermark-free way to save Instagram videos to your device.

Updated June 19, 2026 6 min read By Download My Video

Why download Instagram Reels?

There are plenty of legitimate reasons:

  • Reposting on TikTok or YouTube Shorts (with credit to the original creator)
  • Editing into compilations for your own content
  • Archiving favorite creators — accounts can be deleted at any time
  • Saving your own Reels if you've lost the original file
  • Watching offline — on flights, in subway tunnels, or in areas with poor signal
  • Sending to friends who don't use Instagram
  • Inspiration libraries for content creators

How to download Instagram Reels in 3 steps

Step 1 — Copy the Reel's link

In the Instagram app: Open the Reel, tap the three dots (•••) in the corner, then tap Copy link.

On instagram.com: Open the Reel, click the three dots, then Copy link. Or just copy the URL from your browser's address bar — it'll look like instagram.com/reel/abc123/.

Step 2 — Paste into the downloader

Go to downloadmyvideo.com and paste the link into the input field at the top. The tool fetches the video data automatically — usually within a couple of seconds.

Step 3 — Click Download

Pick MP4 quality (Best Quality is recommended) and click Download. The video saves directly to your device as a clean MP4 — no Instagram logo, no username overlay, no watermarks.

Downloading Instagram Stories

Stories work the same way, but with a small twist: Stories disappear after 24 hours, so you need to grab the link before the story expires. To get a story link:

  1. Open the story in the Instagram app
  2. Tap the three dots (•••) and select Copy link
  3. Paste into the downloader and click Download

Note: you can only download Stories from public accounts. Private accounts hide stories from anyone who isn't a follower, and downloaders can't bypass that.

Downloading regular Instagram videos and IGTV

Same exact process. Any Instagram URL works:

  • Reels: instagram.com/reel/...
  • Posts: instagram.com/p/...
  • IGTV (now merged into video): instagram.com/tv/...

Carousel posts with multiple videos download the first video by default. For specific images or videos in a carousel, swipe to the one you want in the Instagram app first, then copy that specific item's link.

Why don't Instagram downloads have watermarks?

Unlike TikTok, Instagram doesn't burn a watermark into the original video file. When you save a Reel directly from Instagram's "Save to camera roll" option (which is only available to the original creator), it adds the Instagram logo. But the actual file stored on Instagram's servers is clean.

Our downloader fetches the original clean file — not the watermarked version. So what you get is exactly what the creator uploaded.

Common issues

"Login required" or "Login or cookies required to access this content"

This usually means the account is private, the content is age-restricted, or Instagram is rate-limiting requests from our servers. For private accounts, there's no workaround — Instagram requires authentication, and downloaders can't bypass privacy settings (nor should they). For rate-limiting, just try again in a minute.

The video downloads but plays without sound

Some Reels use Instagram's music library. If the music is copyrighted, Instagram sometimes serves the video without audio when accessed via API. You can try a different quality, or look for the same content on the creator's other platforms.

Download is much smaller than I expected

Instagram videos are usually small — a 60-second Reel is typically 5–15 MB at 1080p. That's normal. They're optimized for fast mobile loading.

Can you download Instagram Reels in HD?

Yes. Most Reels are uploaded in 1080p (1920×1080) or 720p, depending on the original device. We give you whatever quality Instagram has stored — usually 720p or 1080p HD. The aspect ratio stays vertical (9:16), so they play perfectly on phones.

Is downloading Instagram videos legal?

For personal use — watching offline, archiving, fair-use editing — it's generally fine in most jurisdictions. Things to avoid:

  • Reposting other creators' content without credit (a quick credit goes a long way)
  • Monetizing content that isn't yours without permission
  • Claiming someone else's Reel as your own
  • Downloading private content you don't have access to

When in doubt: ask the creator. Most creators are happy to give permission for personal use or even reposting, as long as you tag them.

Tips for content creators

If you're a content creator using downloaded Reels for inspiration or reposts:

  • Always credit the original creator — tag them in the caption and link to their account
  • Add value — don't just repost; add your commentary, reaction, or remix
  • Check copyright — even if Instagram allows it, music copyright still applies
  • Build a swipe file — save inspiration in folders organized by niche
  • Use Best Quality (auto) when downloading — gives you the most flexibility for editing
